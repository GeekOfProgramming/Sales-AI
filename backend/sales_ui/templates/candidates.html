Lifecycle Invariants

  • Active Work: Default view of actionable candidates (New, Needs Research, Needs Verification, Needs Correction).
  • Qualified Leads Separation: Once ICP-qualified, candidates leave Active Work and move to Qualified Leads.
  • Historical Reconciliation: Contacted/replied companies are placed in Historical Review Pending until finalized by an operator.
  • Explicit Reopening: Completed, archived, or rejected records can be explicitly reopened back into Active Work.

Dimensional Separation

  • Discovery vs Research: Discovery status tracks crawler ingestion; Research Snapshot captures immutable web evidence.
  • Research vs Verification: AI extractions remain proposals until human-verified in the Lead Register.
  • Safety Invariants: Historical contact suppression and DNC protections remain authoritative regardless of queue view.
