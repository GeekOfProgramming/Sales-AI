Discovery & Triage Statuses

  • New: Newly ingested candidate record awaiting operator review.
  • Needs Research: Target requires deeper company and website research.
  • Watchlist: Candidate saved for observation; paused from active pipeline.
  • Qualified for Enrichment: Meets ICP criteria for deep profile research.
  • Known Company: Company already exists in historical database.
  • Re-engagement Review: Past contact requires manual re-evaluation.
  • Rejected: Candidate does not fit ICP or business requirements.

Research & Contact Invariants

  • Researched: An immutable, persisted Research Snapshot exists. Does not imply all 13 fields are complete, human-approved, or outreach-eligible.
  • Not Researched: No offline snapshot available yet.
  • Contact Availability: Differentiates Verified, Likely, and Unverified emails. Likely emails are never represented as verified.
  • Separation of Concerns: Discovery status, ICP qualification, research completion, contact verification, and manual outreach eligibility are distinct lifecycle states.
